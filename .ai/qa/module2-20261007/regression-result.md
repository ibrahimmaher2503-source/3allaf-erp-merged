# Module 2 — BUG-105 Product Import

- **BUG:** Confirmed. Canonical company XLSX uploads staged but had no usable mapping step; exact-header matching rejected the intended mapped workflow. Approval also permitted cross-company `UpdateExisting` writes and could overwrite edits made after mapping.
- **ROOT CAUSE:** `StageProductImportAction` required canonical headers and globally queried `item_code` during mapping and approval. It captured no target version. The importer also evaluated optional cost under the approver's permission, which could widen a creator's ability to submit cost data.
- **FIX:** Added source-column mapping and clear canonical-template guidance, row-level validation with valid-row-only approval, blank optional defaults, and transactional `CreateOnly` identity rechecks. The original stage company is stored on each staged row using a reserved key that normalized workbook headers cannot produce; mapping and approval reject missing, mixed, changed, or mismatched bindings, including empty batches. `UpdateExisting` resolves through `availableToCompany`, stores the target version during mapping, and rechecks company context, product scope, and lock version at approval. The import page and company XLSX template are available to existing create or edit permission holders; each mode and cancel action still checks its corresponding permission and batch ownership. Cost data is removed during mapping when the creator lacks `cost_view`. CSV is explicitly rejected with a clear message.
